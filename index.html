Why we're starting from zero

Most AI work builds on top of someone else's foundation. MindIndigo is taking the longer road: designing, training and testing our own models end to end, so we understand every layer of what we release.

We're early, and we'd rather be honest about that than overpromise.

Built, not borrowed

Our models are developed in-house from the first line of code.

Open about progress

We'll say what works, what doesn't, and what's still being figured out.

Made to be useful

Every model should solve a real problem for real people.
